How it works

  1. Pick your slices. Mix cheese and pepperoni any way you like.
  2. Choose a pickup time. Lunch or dinner windows, every 30 minutes.
  3. Pay at the window. Cash or card when you grab the box. We text you when it is ready.

Questions

Is it really just $6?

Yes. The price on the menu is the price you pay. No service fee, no tax added at checkout.

Can I order for a group?

Up to 50 of each slice per order. For more than that, text us a day ahead.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. Pickup only at the side door on Elm Street.

What if I'm late?

We hold orders for 30 minutes past your slot, then they go to the walk-up line.
